Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective for www.nayaasoft.com

1. Information We Collect

When you visit www.nayaasoft.com, request a proposal, or contact us through our website or direct communication channels, we collect information you provide directly, such as your full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, company name, and project requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to:

  • Review project requirements and provide accurate proposals and timelines.
  • Communicate with you regarding our web and mobile app development services.
  • Execute agreed-upon software engineering deliverables and customer support.
  • Maintain site security and prevent fraudulent interactions.

3. Client Confidentiality & NDA

We treat all proprietary product concepts, software architecture ideas, wireframes, and business specifications with the highest level of confidentiality. We are always happy to execute a bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before detailed requirement disclosures.

4. Third-Party Services & Cookies

We may use standard analytical cookies or cloud infrastructure tools (e.g. Cloudflare, Google Analytics) to monitor website performance, load times, and traffic sources. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details to third-party marketing companies.

5. Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols, including HTTPS/TLS encryption across all communications, secure credential management, and role-based access control for client databases and repositories.

6. Your Rights & Contact

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your contact data stored in our systems. For any privacy inquiries, email us at hello@nayaasoft.com.
