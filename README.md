---

## 🚀 Quick Preview
1. Double-click **`index.html`** in this folder to open it in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox.
2. No Node.js, Python server, or command line required! It is ultra-lightweight, 100% static HTML, modern CSS, and vanilla JS.

---

## 📁 Project Architecture & Files
- **`index.html`**: Homepage with Hero, Live Interactive Tech Mockup, Services, Instant Budget Estimator, Featured Case Studies, Process, Reviews, and FAQs.
- **`web-development.html`**: Dedicated Web Development service page (Next.js, React, Node.js, E-Commerce, SaaS).
- **`mobile-app-development.html`**: Dedicated Mobile App Development service page (Flutter, iOS & Android, Firebase).
- **`ui-ux-design.html`**: UI/UX & Figma Product Design services.
- **`portfolio.html`**: Interactive portfolio showcase with live category filtering.
- **`our-process.html`**: 6-step transparent development roadmap.
- **`why-choose-us.html`**: Agency comparison matrix (NayaaSoft vs Freelancers vs Legacy Agencies).
- **`about.html`**: Agency story, mission, vision, and core values.
- **`contact.html`**: Interactive project inquiry form, instant WhatsApp link, and direct contact details.
- **`privacy-policy.html` & `terms-and-conditions.html`**: Complete professional legal documentation.
- **`assets/css/`**: Modern design system (`style.css`) and responsive tablet/mobile styling (`responsive.css`).
- **`assets/js/`**: Interactive scripts (`main.js`, `portfolio.js`).
- **`assets/logos/` & `assets/images/`**: Modern vector logo mark and OpenGraph social share card.

## 🛠️ How to Customize Your Info
To replace the placeholder phone number or WhatsApp:
1. Search for `923000000000` in the files and replace it with your actual Pakistani / international WhatsApp number (e.g., `923XXXXXXXXX` without `+` or spaces for `wa.me` links).
2. The contact form validates all fields and either submits to your Formspree/backend URL (if set in `data-endpoint=""`) or automatically redirects the visitor directly to WhatsApp with their inquiry pre-filled!

## 🌐 Deploying to www.nayaasoft.com
You can deploy this site in under 2 minutes for **free** to any modern static host:
1. **Cloudflare Pages / Vercel / Netlify**: Simply drag and drop this entire `nayaasoft` folder, then connect your custom domain `www.nayaasoft.com`. Free SSL, global CDN, and lightning speed are automatic.
2. **cPanel / Shared Hosting**: Upload all files inside the `public_html` directory of your cPanel.
